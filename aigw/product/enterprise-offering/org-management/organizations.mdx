---
title: "Organisations"
description: "Understand the role and features of Organisations, the highest level of abstraction in Prisma AIRS AI Gateway's structure."
---

Organisations in the AI Gateway represent the highest level of abstraction within an account. They serve as containers for all users, entities, and workspaces (if the feature is enabled) associated with your company or project.

Key aspects of Organisations:

* **Comprehensive Scope:** An organisation encompasses all the users and entities within it, providing a broad view of your entire operation.
* **Workspace Support:** Organisations can contain workspaces, which act as sub-organisations for more granular team management and project scoping.
* **Hierarchical Roles:** Organisations have owners and org admins who have access to all workspaces and can create and use admin keys.
* **Multi-Org Flexibility:** the AI Gateway offers an org switcher, allowing users to switch between different organisations they have access to.
* **Security Features:** SSO (Single Sign-On) settings are applied at the organisation level, enhancing security and user management.
* **Private Cloud Integration:** When deploying a gateway in a private cloud, the connection to the control panel is established at the organisation level.

Organisations provide a robust framework for managing large-scale AI development projects, enabling efficient resource allocation, access control, and team management across your entire operation.
